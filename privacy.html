Block Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the Block Rush mobile game ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Block Rush is made by Nizar Bakali, an independent developer.

Short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, or any account. There is no sign-up.
  • Your scores, coins, levels, themes and settings are saved only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to serve and measure ads.
  • Purchases (like "Remove ads") are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device: coins, best scores, level stars, statistics, achievements, unlocked themes, sound and vibration settings, and whether you bought "Remove ads". This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads, and video ads you can choose to watch for rewards. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA)
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system and language
  • How you interact with ads (for example, views and taps)

Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized based on this information. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can limit personalized ads at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or opt out of ads personalization).
  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In-app purchases

If you buy "Remove ads", the payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your name, card number or other payment details. The store only tells the app that the purchase was made, so ads can be turned off. See the Google Privacy Policy or Apple Privacy Policy for how they handle your payment information.

Children

Block Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not collect any ourselves. The only third parties that receive information through the app are Google (AdMob and Google Play) and Apple (App Store), as described above.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device and is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Data collected by Google or Apple is kept according to their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we don't hold any personal data ourselves, these requests are best made to Google or Apple, or by using the ad settings above. You can always contact us for help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
